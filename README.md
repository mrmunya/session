## 🚀 DEPLOY

After forking the repository, deploy it on your preferred hosting platform.

### Steps

1. 🍴 **Fork** the repository.
2. ⭐ **Star** the repository.
3. 🚀 **Deploy** it on your preferred hosting service.
4. 🔐 Generate your WhatsApp session.
5. 📁 Save the generated `creds.json` or `SESSION_ID` securely.
6. 🤖 Add it to your WhatsApp bot.

## 🔐 SECURITY

**Never share your `SESSION_ID` or `creds.json` with anyone.**

Anyone with access to your session credentials may be able to access your WhatsApp bot account.
